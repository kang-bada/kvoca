Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

K-voca is a personal desktop tool run by its owner on the owner's own computer. It makes short vocabulary videos and publishes them to the owner's own social media accounts through each platform's official API. K-voca has no servers, no user sign-up and no other users.

1. What K-voca accesses

When the owner connects an account, K-voca asks only for the permissions below and uses them only for the purpose shown.

PlatformPurposePermissions
YouTubeUpload videos to the owner's channelyoutube.upload
InstagramPublish Reels to the owner's professional accountinstagram_basic, instagram_content_publish
FacebookPublish Reels to the owner's Pagepages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts
TikTokSend videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as draftsuser.info.basic, video.upload
ThreadsPublish text posts with a link on the owner's profilethreads_basic, threads_content_publish
PinterestCreate video Pins on the owner's boardboards:read, pins:read, pins:write

K-voca does not read followers, messages, comments, contacts or any other people's information.

2. What is stored and where

  • Access tokens from the platforms are stored only in a settings folder on the owner's computer, readable only by the owner's user account. They are never sent to any server run by K-voca (there is none).
  • Videos, captions and upload results (post IDs and links) are kept on the owner's computer and optional external drive.
  • K-voca does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. These web pages do not either.

3. What is shared

  • Videos and captions are sent to the platform the owner chose, to be published there.
  • Vocabulary words (not personal data) may be sent to an AI service to write example sentences.
  • Review messages about K-voca's own videos are sent to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • K-voca never sells, rents or shares data with anyone else.

4. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects an account. To delete everything, the owner removes K-voca's data folder. Access can also be revoked at any time from each platform:

  • Google / YouTube: Google account permissions
  • Instagram, Facebook, Threads: Settings → Apps and websites / Business integrations
  • TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps

5. YouTube API Services

K-voca uses YouTube API Services. By using K-voca's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

6. Children

K-voca is not directed to children and does not collect data from anyone.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page. Questions: kvoca.daily@gmail.com.
