One minute a day, new words with native audio

English · Japanese · Chinese · Korean for learners · Beginner, intermediate and advanced

K-voca is a small personal project that makes short vocabulary videos: a few words a day, each read by a native voice with its meaning and an example sentence. The videos are published on K-voca's own channels.

The K-voca desktop tool is used only by its owner to publish K-voca's own videos to K-voca's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.
